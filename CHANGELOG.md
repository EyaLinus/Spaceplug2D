# Changelog

## v0.1.13 – 2026-11-06
- **Main quest chapter 3 - the Ninth Veil:** six new missions chained after Cold Front. Whispers on Varrow, Cheap Faith (take a street from the Veil), The Quiet Toll (survive a Veil interdiction), Lanterns Out (shoot down 4 Veil ships), Veil Spire (destroy their base, with waypoint) and Quill's Ledger (settle Inspector Brandt's audit). Givers: Rhea Dunmore, Captain Jex Ferro, Ines Okoro, Pavo Brandt. Credit rewards from 400 to 3,000 cr.
- **Surface art pass (slice 1):** every planet's ground tileset now has 4 rare feature tiles on top of the 4 plain ones (flowers, ore, cracks, hatches, cables, bones, frost) and the lawn, dune, salt, ice and rubble areas use real paint tiles instead of tinted rectangles. Buildings get three roof and three wall surfaces (shingles, plates, brick, planks) picked per building, lit/shuttered/dark windows, an eave shadow, a striped awning over service doors and rooftop gear (vents, skylights, chimneys, dishes). Built via `tools/art/build_art.py`; `tools/shot.gd` now shoots every planet.
- **Sealing Bench (P4):** a new estate building (320 cr, 2x1, Build menu) that re-wraps finished product from your hold into Sealed or Shielded packs. Wrap supplies cost 4 / 10 cr per unit, 45 / 90 s of game time, 30 units per bench; the packs wait on the bench until you take them, keep their grade and traits, and stack separately in the hold (`scripts/core/sealing.gd`, state saved, old saves load empty). Needs the packaging tier keys of the packaging-tiers PR to have an effect in patrol scans.
- **Autopilot avoids obstacles:** the ship autopilot now steers around planets, asteroid outposts and stations on the way to the waypoint, and detours around Authority patrol scan zones while you carry contraband.
- **Third star system, Thessaly Drift:** a content pack (`data/system_thessaly.json`, merged idempotently by `Data.merge_pack`) adds a system two jumps out (Halvane > Kovar > Thessaly) with Quillon (rich, heavy security, patrol base) and Ashfall (lawless moon, cheap raws, fence), each with its own demand, heat per sale and security. Jump gates can carry their own `toll` and `seconds`; the Kovar-Thessaly lane costs 140 cr and 30 s. The Map app shows the system and sums the tolls of multi-hop routes. Old saves load unchanged.
- **Idle chatter:** people on planet surfaces now say short speech-bubble lines when you walk past, flavoured by planet and time of day (data/chatter.json).
- **Cartel price wars:** a cartel that rules a location can start a four-day price war there: you earn 30 % less when selling and pay 25 % more when buying. The HUD flags it and the Map app lists it with a "Pay off" button; raising your influence, wrecking a cartel base or waiting also ends it.
- **Visible workers:** hired lab hands and stockers now walk around the estate you are standing in: to the storage crate to fetch or drop off goods, to their station while a batch cooks, and to an idle spot by the door when there is nothing to do. They wear their own recoloured outfit and show their first name. Purely cosmetic, production is unchanged.
- **Drying Table (P4):** a new estate building (300 cr, 2x1, Build menu) that cures finished weed over game time: hang units on the rack, 120 s later they come off one quality grade higher but 20 % lighter. Racks hold 20 units per table, state is saved (`scripts/core/drying.gd`, old saves load empty), with tooltip, pixel sprite and tests.
- **Running away raises heat:** sprinting in an officer's sight adds heat per second, more while an officer is chasing you (or has drawn a weapon) and less when you merely carry product. Out of sight or walking adds nothing. Rates and cap are in `content.json` -> `heat` (`flee_chase_per_second`, `flee_carry_per_second`, `flee_heat_cap`).
- **Map marker:** In the Map app you can mark any location ("Mark") or star system ("Set marker") and clear it again. With no quest waypoint active, the ship autopilot (T) and the HUD arrow lead to the marker (a marker in another system routes to the jump gate); quest waypoints keep priority, and the autopilot switches off on arrival. Saved; old saves load without one.
- **Estate building tiers:** the global Hydro Rig upgrade is now a production-line tier per estate (rebuild it from the shipyard tab). Old saves keep their rig level on the Old Cannery.
- **Hidden crate:** your estates get a hidden crate (Stash tab). Product you hide there is safe from patrol busts and knock-outs. It starts with room for 40 units and can be upgraded three times (+40 each).
- **Hiding spots:** Each planet has four shaded hiding spots (dumpsters, crate stacks, alcoves). Stand still in one for under a second and officers can no longer see you: no frisk, and anyone chasing or aiming at you gives up after two seconds. An officer who walks right up to you finds you, and shooting, being shot or moving breaks cover.
- **Planet laws:** every planet now rates each drug tier legal, tolerated or banned (new `laws` data and `scripts/core/laws.gd`). Legal goods halve sale heat and patrol scans; banned goods add heat, widen scans and frisks, but sell at a +20% black-market premium. Shown in the Map app (laws line and tooltip per location). Numbers are eyeballed.
- **Localisation foundation (DE/EN):** string tables in `data/loc/en.json` and `de.json`, looked up with `Loc.t(key, fallback)` (`scripts/core/loc.gd`); missing keys fall back to English. New language selector (English / Deutsch) in Settings > Display, saved in `settings.cfg`. Main menu, pause menu and settings screen are fully translated and switch live; all other screens stay English until they are converted.
- **Grow trays:** build grow trays in your estates (Build menu), plant cannabis or coca from your hold, water them once a day and harvest into the stash after a few days. Watering every day gives the full yield, neglect halves it. Old saves load without trays.
- **Grow trays, second slice:** your grow trays are now drawn in the estate room (plants grow with the days, gold frame when ripe), Pseudoephedrine is a third crop, and harvested raws carry a quality grade (poor/good/prime) that blends with what is already in the stash.
- **Courier drones:** buy drones in the HR app (estate screen) to fly finished product from an estate to the hub stash at the spaceport, or supplies for the auto recipe back to the estate. Upkeep per day, trips can be shot down at high heat.
- **Mixing journal:** the Mixing Station now lists the mixes you already cooked on this drug, newest first, with a one-click Re-cook button (greyed out with the reason when additives are short). The result preview also shows each trait's value (for example Overclock +35%). Old saves load fine.
- **Customers on foot:** on a planet, townsfolk and contacts who want what you carry walk up to you with a speech bubble ("2 × Weed? 48 cr"). Talk to them to open a small deal card: Accept, Ask +10 % / +20 % (one haggle) or Walk away. The price is your asking price, and the sale goes through the normal sell path, so heat, cartels and quests react as before.
- **Packaging tiers:** the Packaging Table score now sets a tier on the batch (Loose, Sealed, Shielded). In a patrol scan each unit of a Sealed (15%) or Shielded (55%) product can stay in your hold instead of being confiscated. The tier shows in the product name and the batch result.
- **Patrol warning:** a toast, red flash and alarm sound tell you when you are about to enter an Authority patrol's scan range (while carrying product).
- **Asteroid fields:** each system has dense fields; inside them patrol scan range shrinks and patrols lose lock and drop the chase.
- **Bust shake in space:** the camera now shakes when you are busted or wrecked in flight (it only did on foot before).
- **Night curfew:** 22:00–05:00 two extra officers patrol and see 30 % further. Press G to bribe a patrol (60 cr + 2 per heat): 3 h pass, heat −10.
- **Planet heat zones:** where you deal on a planet now matters. Sales and hand-overs in the open plaza make 160 % of the normal heat, on the roads 100 %, in the back alleys off the roads only 60 %. The district label above the minimap shows your zone and its heat factor.
- **Estate raids:** when a system's heat stays high for several days, the Authority can raid one of your estates there. You get a warning a day ahead (a hired patrol captain lowers the odds and tips you off), so empty the stash and the tray in time. A raid seizes half the estate stock and tray, fines you, cools the system and raises your wanted level.
- **Price board:** the game now remembers the buy prices (raws, additives) and plain-batch sell values you saw at each location, one note per game day while docked, last 14 days, saved with the game. The Map app's price comparison shows a trend arrow and a sparkline per location with the last days as a tooltip.
- **Building interiors:** The key buildings on every planet (Mireth hangar, Night Market and Consortium office, Cinder depot, Hull Market and the Back Room, Salt Market and cantina, Glacis exchange) now have a small room of their own. Press E at the door to walk in; the counter and the clerk open the same panel the door used to, and the exit puts you back on the street. Estates work as before.
- **Escort missions:** take a freight job in the Quests app while docked (Mireth, Cinder, Bazaar, Varrow), undock and fly the freighter to its destination, through the Kovar gate on the long charters. Cartel wings and raiders go for it; deliver it with a healthy hull for the full bonus, lose it and the job fails.
- **Automation machines:** three new estate buildings run a production step for you, no minigame and no worker: the Auto-Trimmer (weed trim), the Drip Mixer (pours the additives you picked) and the Packing Machine (seals and packs). Use them like a table, pay a few credits per unit; they score steady Standard-grade results, below a good hand. Machines stand in for the table when an estate is checked for a recipe and lab hands use them too. Find them in the new Machines section of the estate Build tab.
- **Art pass 4:** new pixel art generated from source grids: icons for all seven lab stations, ground weapons, combat HUD (hull, shield, missile, boost) and galaxy map markers; sprites for laser/pirate bolts, homing missile, explosion, hit spark and shield bubble; idle animation sheets (breathing, blinking) for every character; road/plaza edge fringes for every ground type; richer starfield sprites and nebula clouds. Most of it is registered for later wiring rather than drawn yet.

## v0.1.11 – 2026-11-03
- **Interdiction (D3):** jumps between systems can now be intercepted. Angry cartels pull you out of hyperspace where they hold ground, and from day 2 onward they target haulers carrying product more often. Reach Authority interceptors stop you when you carry product and your heat is high. A card gives you four options: pay the toll or a bribe (a bribe also lowers your heat), dump your product (the cartel gains ground from it), run for it (the chance depends on your engine and hull class, and failing costs hull) or fight. Fighting the Authority raises your heat, and shooting down an interceptor pays nothing.
- **Main quest chapter 2:** four new missions after the debt is paid. Turf War: own a location. Toll Road: get through an interdiction. Smoke the Nest: raid the Black Comets' base. Cold Front: raid the Saltglass Tide's rig in Kovar.
- **Space combat II (E4):** cartels now fly wings at you: tinted fighters, and armoured gunships that fire three-shot fans once a cartel is angry enough. Every downed cartel ship costs that cartel ground in the system. Each cartel has a base you can raid (Comet Nest in Halvane, Saltglass Rig and Veil Spire in Kovar). Bases have four turrets and launch defenders. Destroying one pays 900 to 1,400 cr, drops loot, takes a big share of the cartel's ground and knocks the cartel out for 3 days. Two new secondary weapons at the shipyard: the Flak Battery (a short-range pellet cone) and the Ion Emitter (stuns ships around the hit and does double damage to gunships and bases). K fires the selected secondary and Q switches between them. Wrecks drop loot crates with credits, missiles, raws, additives or product, and you pick them up by flying over them. The shipyard sells hull classes: Runner (fast, small hold), Hauler (big hold, slow) and Corvette (armour, shield, hotter lasers). You can switch between hulls you own for free.
- **Enemy cartels and sector influence (I1):** three rival cartels now fight you for the streets: the Black Comets (Halvane), the Saltglass Tide and the Ninth Veil (both Kovar), each with a boss, a colour and a home system. Every trade location has influence shares (you, each cartel, unclaimed street). Your sales and your dealers' sales take ground, shooting cartel ships costs them ground in that system, and every morning the cartels push back. Holding a location pays up to 15% better prices and keeps raiders away; on cartel turf your dealers get robbed. Turf changes show as notifications. The Map app has a new Influence overlay with per-location share bars and a Cartels screen with each cartel's boss, mood and hostility.

## v0.1.8 – 2026-11-03
- **Deal appointments:** customers now text a concrete deal with the product (often one from your recipe book), amount, price and a meeting time on the clock at a place. Hand over from two hours before the meeting; late costs affinity, on time earns some, and after the window the deal is missed. Requests use your asking price when the customer accepts it.
- **Haggle on everything:** before you agree, haggle once each on price (+10/+25 %), amount (fewer or more) and time (2 h earlier or 3 h later).
- **Calendar:** the Com-Phone home screen lists today's meetings, and tapping one opens the conversation. You get a reminder an hour before each meeting.
- **Dealers:** hire street dealers in the HR app (one-off fee, then they keep a cut). Give them a district, hand them product in person, and they sell to the locals at your asking price while you're away. Collect the cash when you're back in their district. Heat raises their bust risk, skill lowers it, and a bust costs their stock and cash and puts them in jail for a while.
- **Auto recipes from your book:** the HR estate screen can now set a worker's auto recipe to any mix from the Products app, not only plain drugs.
- **Products app:** a recipe book on the Com-Phone. Every product you've cooked once, grouped by drug, with its recipe in mixing order, traits, best grade and the estates that can make it; drugs you never cooked show as "???".
- **Your own prices:** set an asking price per product (grades scale it) or leave it on Market. Customers pay your price if it is within what they'd pay (loyal and hooked customers tolerate more), refuse if it is too steep, and like you a bit more for a bargain. The product screen lists every buyer you know with their offer and limit.

Hyperspace streaks while charging and a hyperspace loading transition on every jump; docking, a bust or the Cancel button interrupt the charge.

Your ship has hull and shield bars (bottom left); the shield recharges after a few quiet seconds and on docking, the hull is repaired for credits at the mechanic. Downed pirates pay a bounty. If your hull is gone the pirates loot half your hold, you pay a tow fine and respawn at half hull next to the system's patrol base.
- **Weapon and shield modules:** the shipyard sells Laser Cannons, Missile Pods, a Shield Generator and Hull Armour (three levels each). Every level shows on the ship as extra wing guns, missile pods, a shield ring and armour plates. New Mechanic section: repair hull, restock missiles. The key bindings are in the settings menu. Old saves start with an undamaged stock ship.

Pistol is free, SMG (600 cr) and shotgun (900 cr) are bought in the wheel. You have 100 HP that regenerates after a few quiet seconds; at 0 you are knocked out (product confiscated, fine, back at your ship). Low cover (barriers and crates) stands along the streets of every planet and stops bullets. Officers draw their weapons at heat 55 or when shot at, stop to fire and can be shot down (extra heat). Saves are backward compatible.
- **DeadDrop app:** request anonymous drop points on planets, stations and outposts. Leave an agreed Deal-O-Matic deal there (30 % of the heat, customer collects and pays later, small theft risk) or send goods to your hub stash by courier.

- **Drug ladder:** products renamed by strength – T1 Weed, T2 Cocaine, and new T3 Meth (Pseudoephedrine, Cinder Belt) and T4 Fentanyl (precursor at the Drift Bazaar). Higher tiers sell for more but cost more and bring up to 2.4× heat.
- **Saves:** v0.1.0 saves load with the new names.

## v0.1.0 – 2026-10-30
- **First playable prototype:** fly a ship around the Halvane Reach, dock at Mireth, Cinder Belt Outpost and Drift Bazaar.
- **Cook & mix:** two base products, four additives that transform and add traits, 13 traits with their own value.
- **Deals:** five contacts with favourite traits and cooldowns, a fence at the Bazaar, demand that saturates and recovers.
- **Heat & patrols:** heat from cooking and selling, more patrols as it rises, chase above 30 heat, scan → bust, bribes.
- **Progress:** cargo, engine, stealth and lab upgrades; pay off 6,000 cr debt by day 12 to win; save/continue.
- **Audio:** synthesized sound effects, engine rumble and ambient drone.
