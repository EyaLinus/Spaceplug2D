- **Enemy cartels and sector influence (I1):** three rival cartels (Ouroboros Consortium, Black Comets, Dust Kings) with home locations, colours and bosses. Every trading location has an influence split between you, the cartels and nobody. Sales (yours and your dealers'), delivered drops and downed raiders raise your share; wrecks, knock-outs and stolen drops lower it, and a lead bleeds back a little each day. High influence pays better, makes raws cheaper and draws fewer pirates; below 8% your dealers get robbed and drops are stolen more often. New influence overlay in the Map app with share bars and a cartel legend. Old saves start at the neutral share.
