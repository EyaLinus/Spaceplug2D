- **Quests (Q1):** data-driven quests in `content.json` that advance on what you do (buy, cook, sell, dock, deliver, talk, estate, jump). A tracker in the HUD shows the current step, a gold waypoint arrow points the way (through the jump gate when it is in another system) and the new Quests app on the phone lists active and finished quests. The tutorial chain walks you from your first Cannabis purchase through the first batch, first sale, first contact, first delivery and estate to the first jump, with small cash rewards. Old saves skip the tutorial.
