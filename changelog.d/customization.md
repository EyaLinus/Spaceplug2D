- **Customization (K2):** make the game look like yours. At any shipyard the new Paint shop & mirror section sells hair colours and jacket colours for your character, ship paint and hull decals (racing stripe, chevrons, star, skull); each option is paid once and then switches for free, and the preview shows your ship. Paint and decals show in flight, on the landing pad and in the shipyard. In any estate's Build menu a new Decoration section sells floor props (rug, plant, lamp, poster, couch, trophy) that you can walk over; up to 24 per estate, removing one refunds half. Everything is cosmetic and old saves load with the factory look.
