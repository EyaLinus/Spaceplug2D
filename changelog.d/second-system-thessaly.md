- **Third star system, Thessaly Drift:** a content pack (`data/system_thessaly.json`, merged idempotently by `Data.merge_pack`) adds a system two jumps out (Halvane > Kovar > Thessaly) with Quillon (rich, heavy security, patrol base) and Ashfall (lawless moon, cheap raws, fence), each with its own demand, heat per sale and security. Jump gates can carry their own `toll` and `seconds`; the Kovar-Thessaly lane costs 140 cr and 30 s. The Map app shows the system and sums the tolls of multi-hop routes. Old saves load unchanged.
