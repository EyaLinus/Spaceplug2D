- **Space combat II (E4):** cartels now fly wings at you: tinted fighters, and armoured gunships that fire three-shot fans once a cartel is angry enough. Every downed cartel ship costs that cartel ground in the system. Each cartel has a base you can raid (Comet Nest in Halvane, Saltglass Rig and Veil Spire in Kovar). Bases have four turrets and launch defenders. Destroying one pays 900 to 1,400 cr, drops loot, takes a big share of the cartel's ground and knocks the cartel out for 3 days. Two new secondary weapons at the shipyard: the Flak Battery (a short-range pellet cone) and the Ion Emitter (stuns ships around the hit and does double damage to gunships and bases). K fires the selected secondary and Q switches between them. Wrecks drop loot crates with credits, missiles, raws, additives or product, and you pick them up by flying over them. The shipyard sells hull classes: Runner (fast, small hold), Hauler (big hold, slow) and Corvette (armour, shield, hotter lasers). You can switch between hulls you own for free.
