- **Dealers & couriers (HR app part 2):** four new people to hire in the HR app. Dealers (Vik, Nell) sell the finished product on an estate's shelf to your known customers in the same system by themselves and keep a cut (15-28 %, the better ones cost more); every trip risks a bust (more at high heat, less with skill) that loses the goods, raises heat and may cost you the dealer. Couriers (Cass, Ines) run a route you pick in the estate screen: estate to hub stash, hub stash to estate (also brings the raws the auto recipe needs) or estate to another estate. A courier on the payroll also makes DeadDrop courier runs cheaper and safer. Crew limit is now 8. Old saves load with no routes.
