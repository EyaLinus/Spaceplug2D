- **Jump interdiction:** pirates or Reach Authority cutters can now pull your jump out of the lane, at a gate or with the jump drive. Lawless lanes (Kovar Expanse), a hold full of product and high heat make it likelier; a cooldown stops chained ambushes. A panel offers four choices (keys 1-4): fight them with your lasers and missiles (the jump goes ahead once they are down, shooting the Authority adds heat), pay a toll or fine, dump cargo (pirates take the whole hold, the Authority only cares about product), or flee (odds rise with engine upgrades and boost energy; a miss costs hull and starts the fight). Interdictions are never saved; docking, a wreck or a bust calls one off.
