- **Escort missions (E4):** take a freighter job in the new Escort app of the Com-Phone (Mireth: convoy to Kovar, Cinder Belt: ore run to the Bazaar), undock and keep the NPC freighter alive while Black Comets / Saltglass wings raid her. She waits at the gate until you jump with her; reward on arrival (+ bonus for hull left), nothing and a fine if she dies, you abandon her or you jump without her. HUD line and toasts, saved mid-job.
