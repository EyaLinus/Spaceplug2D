- **Main quest chapter 3 - the Ninth Veil:** six new missions chained after Cold Front. Whispers on Varrow, Cheap Faith (take a street from the Veil), The Quiet Toll (survive a Veil interdiction), Lanterns Out (shoot down 4 Veil ships), Veil Spire (destroy their base, with waypoint) and Quill's Ledger (settle Inspector Brandt's audit). Givers: Rhea Dunmore, Captain Jex Ferro, Ines Okoro, Pavo Brandt. Credit rewards from 400 to 3,000 cr.
